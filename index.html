QUICK FIELD GUIDE

Build a network that works.

  1. Choose a line color from the control dock.
  2. Click stations in the order you want trains to visit them.
  3. Connect at least two stations. A train will start running automatically.
  4. Watch passenger dots. They show where people are waiting.
  5. Connect busy places and avoid letting one station become overloaded.

Tip: one well-connected interchange can be more useful than three isolated lines.
